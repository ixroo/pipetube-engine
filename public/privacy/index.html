PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 2026

1. ZERO-DISK & ZERO PERSONAL DATA RETENTION

At PipeTube, user privacy is our foundational architectural principle. We do NOT require users to register, create accounts, or provide personal credentials (such as email addresses, real names, or phone numbers) to use the downloader. Video and audio streams are piped through ephemeral memory buffers and are never stored or retained on disk.

2. LOGGING & RATE LIMITING

To prevent system abuse, DDoS attacks, and bot automated flooding, our servers record transient rate-limiting counters in memory (such as IP hash and timestamp). These logs are strictly used for network security and automatic spam filtering, and are automatically purged. We do not sell or monetize IP addresses.

3. COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE

PipeTube uses minimal browser storage solely for functional purposes:

  • Local Storage (`pipetube_lang`): Stores your selected display language so your preference persists across sessions.
  • Session Cookies (`dl_token_*`): Ephemeral token used solely to notify the browser when a stream download has successfully initiated, automatically expired upon completion.

4. THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING PARTNERS

We may partner with third-party advertising networks (such as Monetag, Adsterra, or Google) to display advertisements on our website. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, and JavaScript to serve relevant ads based on non-personally identifiable browsing patterns. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your browser settings or AboutAds.info.

5. GDPR & CCPA COMPLIANCE

Under European (GDPR) and Californian (CCPA) privacy regulations, users have the right to access, rectify, or erase personal data. Because PipeTube does not store personal data or link IP records to personal profiles, there is no personal profile retained in our databases.

6. CONTACT US

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact Page or by emailing privacy@pipetube.my.id.
