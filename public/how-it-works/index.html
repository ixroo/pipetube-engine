⚡ TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

WHAT IS ZERO-DISK DIRECT STREAMING?

Traditional video downloaders download the entire multi-gigabyte video onto their server's hard disk first, convert it, and then send it to you. This causes high server load, long queue times, and major privacy risks.

PipeTube uses an asynchronous Rust-based streaming pipe: media bytes flow in real-time through volatile RAM memory directly to your browser download manager. Nothing is written to physical hard disks, ensuring:

  • Zero Waiting: Downloads start instantly with no rendering lag.
  • 100% Privacy: No video or audio files are ever saved or stored on our servers.
  • Maximum ISP Speed: Piped at the full bandwidth rate of your internet connection.
