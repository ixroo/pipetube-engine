WHY DO OTHER DOWNLOADERS FAIL AT 1080P AUDIO? (AND HOW PIPETUBE SOLVES IT)

If you have used other YouTube converters, you have probably noticed that videos downloaded in 1080p, 2K, or 4K frequently have no sound, or the site forces you to settle for blurry 720p.

This happens because YouTube uses adaptive DASH streaming: high-definition video feeds above 720p are served as pure video tracks with audio stripped out. Low-tier sites cannot mux them without freezing their servers.
