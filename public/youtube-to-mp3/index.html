What is the difference between MP3 and M4A audio formats? ▼
MP3 is the universal standard supported by virtually every legacy and modern player in existence. M4A (AAC codec) is a modern container that provides slightly cleaner sound fidelity at smaller file sizes, especially on Apple devices (iOS Safari, macOS QuickTime).
