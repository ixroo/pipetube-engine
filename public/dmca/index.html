DMCA & COPYRIGHT POLICY

COMPLIANCE WITH 17 U.S.C. § 512

1. SAFE HARBOR & TECHNICAL NATURE

PipeTube respects the intellectual property rights of creators, artists, and copyright holders. PipeTube operates strictly as a real-time streaming pipe protocol and tool. We do NOT host, store, index, or maintain media files on any server. The content accessed through our service resides entirely on external, third-party public platforms (such as YouTube).

2. REMOVAL OF CONTENT AT THE SOURCE

Because PipeTube does not store files on disk, we have no technical ability to delete copyrighted material from third-party hosting networks. If you are a copyright owner seeking removal of media from the internet, you must file a takedown request directly with the hosting provider (e.g., YouTube's copyright team).

3. URL BLOCKING & TAKEDOWN PROCEDURE

If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and wish to block specific YouTube URLs from being converted or streamed via PipeTube, please submit a written notification containing the following details to our designated agent:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  2. The exact YouTube URL(s) you wish to block from our converter service.
  3. Your contact information (name, organization, email address, physical address, and telephone number).
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, under penalty of perjury, and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
  6. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent.

4. DESIGNATED AGENT CONTACT

Please direct all DMCA notices and blocking requests to our designated agent:

AGENT: PipeTube Copyright Compliance Office

DOMAIN: pipetube.my.id
